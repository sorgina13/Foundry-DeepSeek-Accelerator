# Foundry DeepSeek Accelerator

Deploy, evaluate and cost DeepSeek models on Microsoft Foundry against your own
workloads — and get a defensible answer on where they fit in your model portfolio.

This accelerator runs on your Azure subscription, against your data, and produces
decision artefacts you can take to your architecture board and your finance team.

## Kits

The accelerator is organized as a series of focused kits. Each kit answers a specific
adoption or production-readiness question and produces evidence you can use in a model
portfolio decision.

**Current release:** [Cost Assessment Kit](kits/cost-assessment-kit/README.md)

Use the Cost Assessment Kit to answer the first commercial question in a model
portfolio decision: what does it save?

## Roadmap

Later releases extend the accelerator to the questions that follow a displacement
decision.

| Release | Question it answers |
|---|---|
| **[Cost Assessment Kit](kits/cost-assessment-kit/README.md)** *(current)* | What does it save? |
| [Flash Displacement Kit](kits/flash-displacement-kit/README.md) | Which workloads can move to Flash, and what does it save? |
| [Pro Reasoning Kit](kits/pro-reasoning-kit/README.md) | Where does DeepSeek V4 Pro compete on complex reasoning and long-context work? |
| [Secure Deployment Kit](kits/secure-deployment-kit/README.md) | How do we run this under our identity, network and data controls? |
| [Scale and Reliability Kit](kits/scale-and-reliability-kit/README.md) | Will it hold at our concurrency, and what does provisioned throughput change? |
| [Governance and FinOps Kit](kits/governance-and-finops-kit/README.md) | How do we attribute, cap and monitor spend per consuming application? |
| [Production Decision Pack](kits/production-decision-pack/README.md) | What is the path to production, and who owns what? |
